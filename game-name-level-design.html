What I Worked On

I made this game in the beginning of my game design major in 2/26/2025 for a class focused on level design at Champlain College. This was a solo project. I used the template from unreal engine and added design elements to create a level for a team based shooter game. Winning requires getting the most eliminations of the Opposing team. The level included pick ups for different weapons as well as healing, platforming challenges, different levels, and portals to travel through. I designed the level itself, the position of players, the portals and the obstacles, as well as the structures, floors and layout of the terrain. The trickiest part was getting the lighting and colors the way I wanted them, in the end I wasnt fully satisfied with that piece.
