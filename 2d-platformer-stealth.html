2D Platformer Stealth

I made this game in the spring of 2026 at Champlain College while studying game design. This was a group project, with three people on the team. My role was lead designer and I also designed and built the level. The other two members focused on programming and art, though we all did a little bit of everything to create the game. The goal of the game is to infiltrate a facility to retrieve a jewel and get out before being detected and attacked by the security systems. One of the team members did not finish the animations, so you will notice the player character is not animated.

Project Details

  • Role: Lead Designer and Level Designer
  • Tools: Unity Engine
  • Team: Three-person team
