What I Worked On

The purpose of the game is to teach students or players about the Baltic Tree Cake (Sakotis) and its place in Lithuanian culture. The game begins on a title screen, after the start button is pressed the player is presented with trivia about Sakotis and Lithuanian culture. Then the player is tasked with making a Sakotis with batter that is poured onto a spit. It ends with a short quiz (trivia) section to measure student learning. This game was made with a team of four students including myself, in the Spring Semester at Champlain College 2026. My role was to be the designer of the game, however, as the game evolved we all worked together to edit and shape the original game which I created, changing the game together. Mostly those changes looked like simplifying the original plan because we realized that with only three weeks we might not be able to accomplish all of the original goals. Other team members worked on programming, art, and sound.
